WORKBENCH GUIDE
A quick tour
Select
Click a part. Shift-click to select several. Highlighted text can also be attached to your chat.
Ask AI
Right-click a part or choose Ask AI. Check the attached context before you send.
Move around
Drag the empty canvas to pan. Drag a part to move it. Scroll to zoom, or use Fit view to see the assembly.
Try a change
Ask for an improvement, review its preview, then choose Apply draft. Undo lets you step back.
VISUAL DRAFT
Drafts change this workspace's diagram. Source files and recorded runs stay unchanged.
Save workspace keeps your layout and notes in a file you can open again.
